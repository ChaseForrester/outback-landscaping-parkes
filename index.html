Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers regarding earthmoving, machine hire, council compliance, and landscaping in Parkes NSW.

Under the Parkes Shire Council and NSW State Environmental Planning Policy (SEPP), retaining walls under 600mm in height (or 1000mm in rural zones) and minor earth fills under 600mm are generally classified as Exempt Development. For larger engineered cuts or boundary walls exceeding 1m, development approval (DA) or a Complying Development Certificate (CDC) may be required. We assist our clients with site contour checks and engineering compliance.

Parkes is famous for reactive red clay and caliche shale hardpan. When wet, uncompacted clay turns to mud; when dry, it shrinks and cracks. We address this by: (1) stripping organic matter, (2) ripping rock hardpans with our hydraulic rock teeth, (3) treating soils with lime or gypsum where required, and (4) laying heavy geotextile filtration fabric beneath roadbase to prevent driveway sinking.

Laser grading pairs a rotating laser transmitter with dual hydraulic receivers on our track loader blade.
